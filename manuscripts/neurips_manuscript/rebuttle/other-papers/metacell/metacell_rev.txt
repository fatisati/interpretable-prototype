Figure 3. Metacell quality metrics.

(A) Purity is defined as the proportion of cells from the most abundant cell type in a metacell. Higher purity corresponds to higher proportion of cells of the same cell type
within a metacell. Purity can also be defined based on other annotations/categories than cell types. (B) Compactness is defined as the average variance of latent space
component within a metacell. Better compactness corresponds to lower variance in the latent space components within cells grouped into a metacell. (C) Separation is
