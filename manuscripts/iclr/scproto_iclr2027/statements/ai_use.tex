\subsection*{AI use statement}

\textcolor{red}{\textbf{[DRAFT --- review and finalise before submission.]}}
In this work, generative AI tools were used as a general-purpose assistant for
writing support (copy-editing and rephrasing of author-written text) and for
coding support (writing and refactoring experiment and analysis scripts).
Generative AI tools were not used to generate research ideas, to derive the
method, or to produce any experimental result or numerical value reported in
this paper. All AI-assisted code was reviewed and tested by the authors, and all
AI-assisted text was reviewed and edited by the authors. We take responsibility
for the final content of this work, including all text, claims, and artifacts.
