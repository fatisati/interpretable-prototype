\section*{AI use statement}

Generative AI tools were used in this work in three
capacities. First, for writing support: copy-editing and rephrasing of
author-written text. Second, for coding and execution support: implementing
and refactoring experiment and analysis scripts, executing the associated
training and evaluation runs, and reading the
resulting output files to report and tabulate the numerical values used in
this paper. Third, for limited mathematical assistance: helping derive and
explore some formula and normalization details of the method during
implementation. All experimental design decisions --- which experiments to
run, which configurations and hyperparameters to use, and which results to
report --- were made by the authors; generative AI tools were not used to
decide the experimental design or to select which results to include. All
AI-executed code, all AI-assisted mathematical derivations, and all
AI-reported experimental results were reviewed and verified by the authors
against the underlying implementation and output files, and all AI-assisted
text was reviewed and edited by the authors. We take responsibility for the
final content of this work, including all text, claims, experimental results,
and artifacts.
