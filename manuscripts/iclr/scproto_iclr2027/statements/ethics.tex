\section*{Ethics statement}

This work uses only publicly available, de-identified single-cell and spatial
transcriptomics datasets, obtained and used in accordance with their original
licences and data-use terms; no new human or animal data were collected and no
IRB approval was required. The method produces aggregated cell profiles
(metacells) for exploratory analysis and is not intended for clinical decision
making. The authors declare no conflicts of interest.
