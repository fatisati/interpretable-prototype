\section*{Reproducibility statement}

The method, objective, and all loss terms are specified in
Section~\ref{sec:method}. Dataset sources and preprocessing are described in
Appendix~\ref{app:datasets}, affinity-graph construction in
Appendix~\ref{app:graph_structure}, and architecture, hyperparameters, and the
training schedule in the training appendix. Evaluation metrics are defined with
explicit formulas where they are introduced, and all reported numbers are given
with standard deviations. Code to reproduce every table and figure will be
made publicly available upon acceptance.
