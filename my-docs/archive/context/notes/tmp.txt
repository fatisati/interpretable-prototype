A few patterns worth flagging directly:
- Full vs. test purity/entropy are nearly identical everywhere (max gap ~0.07, most ≤0.03) — the held-out cells aren't behaving very differently from the training cells, which is a good sign the NH placement (frozen-encoder forward pass) isn't degenerate.
- Batch entropy: scPoli-NH wins on all three datasets, by a clear margin. This is the flip side of the purity story — scPoli-NH mixes batches into metacells more (higher entropy) and does it more correctly (higher purity), while scProto mixes less and SEACells barely mixes at all (entropy 0.15-0.41 — SEACells isn't batch-correcting, so its "purity" advantage on lung/pancreas is partly just not attempting cross-batch integration in the first place).
- Immune is scProto's best relative showing — wins 3 of 4 rare metrics outright and is competitive on purity/entropy, consistent with the significance tests from earlier (immune was the one dataset where scProto's purity deficit wasn't statistically significant).
